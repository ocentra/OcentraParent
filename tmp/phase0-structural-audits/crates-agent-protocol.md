- Responsibility: `crates/agent-protocol` is the Rust wire-contract boundary for cross-language payloads, schema constants, and parity tests; the crate is still broader than that because the root module exposes almost the entire contract surface at once.

- Decouple recommendation: separate pure wire-shape definitions from helper/adaptor code, and keep `mod.rs` and `pub use` frontage out of the owning modules unless the file is truly the contract family root.
