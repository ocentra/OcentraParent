# SOCIAL-20 Security Negative Proof

SOCIAL-20 rejects parent social dashboard UX overclaims:

- rendered portal UI;
- notification delivery;
- runtime data fetch;
- policy decisions;
- native app control;
- connector authorization;
- enforcement.

The contracts describe dashboard structure only. They do not fetch data, render
UI, notify parents or children, approve connectors, decide policy, or execute
actions.

The text tokens avoid surveillance, hidden connector, and guaranteed blocking
language. They label review/manual-required states without claiming platform
connector access, native app control, policy decisions, or enforcement.
