# WP05 No-Claim Boundary

Allowed claim: Windows app/game dispatch and timer expiry are gated by the
persisted runtime/session evidence represented in this packet.

Not claimed:

- launcher-only, unknown, stale, or missing session evidence may not execute;
- portal timers are not an authority source;
- AI classification is not an authority source;
- broad process blocking, non-session action paths, mobile parity, and
  notification delivery remain manual-required or follow-on work.
