# Authority Tier Proof

WP31 preserves the authority boundary from WP30.

- Approval authority rows are storage/projection evidence only.
- Inactive or observe-only authority rows cannot grant approve, deny, extend, or
  override capability.
- Manual-required platform rows cannot execute adapters.
- Stored classifier output is evidence only and cannot request action.

No platform row is promoted beyond manual-required or not-claimed state by this
workpack.
