# Manual Platform Proof

No claim stronger than manual-required, unavailable, or not-claimed was made in
this workpack.

Manual proof still required before any broad app/game blocking upgrade:

- Windows: AppLocker or App Control setup, enforce apply, rollback, audit
  custody, and safe system-app allowlist proof.
- macOS: MDM/profile, Endpoint Security, or System Extension setup plus
  rollback and audit proof.
- Linux: named distro, session, mechanism, service/admin setup, rollback, and
  audit proof.
- Android: Device Owner or Profile Owner setup plus hide/suspend apply,
  rollback, and audit proof.
- iOS/iPadOS: FamilyControls authorization, ManagedSettings shield or
  supervised/MDM proof, rollback/exception proof, and audit proof.
