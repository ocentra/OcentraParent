# Authority Tier Proof

Authority tier: observe/query-store only.

WP35 does not move any app/game row into a stronger platform authority tier. The
recurring service capture path records runtime evidence and keeps foreground,
policy, adapter, blocking, suspension, shielding, uninstall, and rollback
authority unclaimed.

Proof needed to move up:

- Foreground source proof for foreground claims.
- Policy decision proof for runtime consumers.
- Platform adapter capability proof for any action.
- Rollback and audit refs before enforcement support is claimed.
