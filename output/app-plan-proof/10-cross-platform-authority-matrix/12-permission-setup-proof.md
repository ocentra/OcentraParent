# Permission And Setup Proof

Permission and setup fields are contract-backed, not runtime-backed.

Rows can represent not-required, permission-required, helper-required,
managed-profile-required, device-owner-required, MDM-required,
supervision-required, system-extension-required, admin/root-required,
kiosk-required, manual-required, and not-claimed states.

No OS setup commands or enrollment were executed in this workpack.
